# Phase 18 independent forecasting findings

## Verified implementation

The platform adds an event-probability consumer to the existing experimental research
archive. It reuses public Polymarket observations, does not duplicate the collector,
and never calls account/order APIs. Existing sports, domains, Phase 16/17 history and
production strategies remain intact. Genuine independent **research estimates** are
separate from calibrated tradable probabilities and certified strategy approval.

The first new model supports exact Bitcoin/Ethereum Binance daily-low threshold contracts.
It matches full resolution text, pair, local New York day/DST window, end time and outcome
side. A daily minimum barrier is not a terminal close. Ambiguous noon-close contracts
remain quarantined. Two bounded market-data-only GETs fetch contiguous completed minutes,
with actual post-response retrieval/availability timestamps and original bytes/hashes.
No price from Polymarket is a model feature. Source denial has no bypass/fallback.

The model estimates lower first-passage under zero-log-drift Brownian log prices with
720 past completed minute log returns. Horizon starts at the last observed close, including
the unknown forming minute. Its constant diffusion, continuous-path approximation, jump
risk, discrete minute-Low convention and volatility regime assumptions remain unvalidated.
Extreme tails may round numerically to0/1; they are not empirical certainty. Every estimate
retains[0,1]uncertainty and `unvalidated_research` status. It cannot establish positive
conservative edge or high conviction. Model/source hashes, features, evidence IDs, rules,
market/book baseline and dataset identity are stored prospectively and never revised.

## Negative results and unresolved hypotheses

No statistically credible event forecasting superiority or executable profitable edge has
been established. New event-disjoint training/heldout counts are0/0, not the old20 temporal
mark episodes. Calibration/reliability/Brier/logloss/confidence intervals and superiority
remain unsupported until independent terminal outcomes and adequate reviewed samples exist.
Existing short-horizon comparison remains operational; its negative after-cost mark evidence
is not relabeled event forecasting or removed from history.

Both throughput/conviction approaches reject uncalibrated single-lineage estimates. Any
future comparison requires forward decisions, genuine current liquidity, all unchanged risk
and strategy-admission approvals, paired independent event evaluation and dependence-aware
uncertainty. Lowering thresholds, selecting successful samples, counting complementary tokens
or treating simulated P/L as independent training evidence cannot solve these deficiencies.
