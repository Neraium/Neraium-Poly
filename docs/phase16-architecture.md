# Phase 16: causal qualification evidence

The modular monolith, existing strategy registry, risk engine, certified approval gate,
and isolated paper accounts remain in place. New models are research-only.

## Evidence path

An existing genuine public market frame feeds an eight-token causal cohort every ten
seconds. Original frame identity, source/receipt times, producer health, market rules,
fee evidence, and observed executable depth accompany each append-only QualificationStudy.
Completed sixty-second bid marks require an actually observed later quote within thirty
seconds of the horizon. Marks are monetary valuations, not terminal outcome probabilities.
Duplicate source timestamps cannot add persistence. Gaps require rewarming.

Dynamic relationship selection uses only information already available at selection.
Three bounded pairs retain versioned selection and retirement reasons. Statistical
persistence does not establish a logical constraint, causal relationship, or trading edge.

A verified deterministic compressed checkpoint is appended every five minutes. Other
studies retain raw inputs and parent/hash references. Recovery replays from the most
recent checkpoint, rejects corruption and source-code changes, and never resets evidence
silently. Compression operates on in-memory checkpoint JSON; open SQLite files are never
compressed or relocated. Existing managed archives retain all original evidence.

## Execution realism and authority

A negative-only operational veto assesses previously eligible original signals. It checks
actual best-limit executable depth separately from a hypothetical multi-level sweep,
minimum lots, quarter-depth participation, current quotes and fees, spread, and conservative
entry/exit costs. It can reject an existing candidate but cannot admit a rejected signal.
Original signal inputs, source code hash, veto assessment and provenance are durable.
Pass/veto counts cover committed emitted signals after signature deduplication; original
strategy tracing continues to count its original pre-veto evaluations. Pass is not risk
approval. New challengers never acquire order authority.

Readiness distinguishes incomplete evidence, defects and cost rejection. A displayed edge
requires matching market/token/event identity, mature per-token history, current source
and receipt timestamps, and passing execution checks. Terminal calibration separately
requires authentic independent outcomes and unchanged 100 training/100 held-out events.

## Outcome collection repair

Official Gamma market detail can omit parent-event metadata. The collector now corroborates
the exact known parent through the official event endpoint, binding condition, question,
rules, ordered tokens/outcomes and observation window. Contradictions reject. Native evidence
remains bounded and replayable. Missing native resolution transaction/reporter/type fields
still block outcome verification; closed markets and terminal prices are not outcomes.

## Operations and retention

`uv run neraium-poly monitor --view readiness` exposes causal coverage, cost readiness,
terminal calibration gaps, paper balances, emergency stop and committed veto/pass counts.
Existing operations views retain capacity forecasts. Collector and experimental budgets
remain 64 GiB and 16 GiB, with existing filesystem floors. No originals are removed, retention
policy changed, infrastructure purchased, stop cleared, or model certified by this phase.
