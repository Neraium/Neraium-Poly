# Historical evidence acquisition

`HistoryPipeline(directory, max_bytes=100*1024*1024, max_requests=400)` downloads public evidence without trading, model training or approval. `await acquire(max_events=250, max_history_requests=50, max_context_requests=5, archive_path=None)` writes immutable SHA-addressed raw responses, a hashed `manifest.json`, normalized `bundle.json` and a completeness report. `load_bundle(directory)` checks both normalized-evidence and raw-payload hashes. Replaying the stored bundle makes no network requests.

The initial selection is deliberately bounded: at most500 closed-market rows from fixed offset windows ordered by closed time. Exactly one binary YES token is retained per parent/event group, avoiding inflation from many correlated legs. This is a convenience sample, not exchange completeness or a representative sample. Sampling provenance, URL parameters, response/cache headers, retrieval time, byte counts and request limits are retained. A rejected source page is recorded as a partial-discovery deficiency. HTTP429/503 retry delays are respected; a delay beyond the bounded immediate retry window stops acquisition instead of retrying early. Download and normalized output sizes are bounded; free disk is checked before acquisition.

Historical chart data uses the official Data API `/v2/prices-history` at coarse12-hour resolution. Each point retains its source timestamp, but its availability remains today's download time. Settlement markers0/1 and post-close points are excluded from the normalized prediction series, while raw responses remain archived. The pipeline never synthesizes past bids, asks, depth, uniform sampling intervals, fills or profits from chart prices. A returned cursor is reported as truncated coverage rather than silently treated as complete.

Final labels require official condition-bound resolved lifecycle data with a transaction reference and no dispute/extended-review flags. Native labels use explicit binary payout arrays. Legacy UMA labels additionally require matching CLOB token identities and exactly one explicit winner flag; displayed token prices are not used to establish outcomes. Unknown resolution timestamps remain unknown, and updated-rule cases are marked for historical applicability review. This is research label corroboration, not permission for an automated portfolio settlement.

`import_books(source_sqlite,max_books=500)` reads a genuine append-only operational archive in read-only mode, verifies payload digests and preserves original exchange/receipt timestamps. It never reassigns current books to historical events. Imported snapshots are copied into a raw artifact so the research bundle does not depend on later mutation of the source database.

Where selected weather rules explicitly identify a METAR station, the pipeline can fetch the US Aviation Weather Center's public METAR observations. These are actual independent external context records, linked to the selected event and source payload. Observation timestamps do not retroactively prove historical receipt: imported context remains `collected_now`. A named research review must establish predecision availability and relevance before context can qualify certification. Unrelated station reports are not silently mapped to another market.

## Actual initial acquisition

On2026-11-06 the bounded official-API acquisition downloaded3,711,202 response bytes in36 GET requests. From500 raw market rows,434 supported binary rows collapsed to21 distinct parent/event groups;66 rows lacked unambiguous YES-token semantics. Seven group outcomes were corroborated,18 groups had67 normalized historical price points, and96 public METAR observations were retained. No historical order-book archive was imported. The bundle contains0 certifiable events and does not establish the required100 training plus100 heldout executable events. The API's offset3000 request was observed to return422; fixed sampling windows0/500/1000/1500/2000 remained within the supported bounded probe. Failed exploratory raw payloads are preserved separately from the final manifest's successful acquisition.

These initial counts do not prove that200 historical outcome groups are globally unavailable. They describe this exact bounded sample. Native/legacy outcome coverage, historical quote availability, independent context and receipt-time proof are separate completeness dimensions. `completeness_report(bundle)` reports each rather than combining price rows or correlated outcomes into independent training samples. Certification feasibility requires matching actual two-sided fresh archived books, verified labels and independently reviewed predecision context; current downloads alone do not satisfy that requirement.

## Primary sources and further archive leads

- [Official Data API V2 specification](https://data-api.polymarket.com/v2/openapi.json): resolution selectors, payout fields and historical price schema. Coarse historical prices are not L2 books.
- [Official Gamma specification](https://docs.polymarket.com/api-spec/gamma-openapi.yaml) and [Polymarket Institute research guide](https://institute.polymarket.com/data): market/event metadata and public data surfaces.
- [US Aviation Weather Center API](https://aviationweather.gov/data/api/): external METAR observations; historical publication/receipt applicability still needs review.
- [PolyOrderbooks publisher datasets](https://polyorderbooks.com/datasets) and [toolkit repository](https://github.com/polyorderbooks/polymarket-orderbook-toolkit): advertised CC BY4.0 captured L2 samples, including805 markets. Market count does not establish independent event-window count or final-label authenticity.
- [DineshKumar8399 publisher repository](https://github.com/DineshKumar8399/polymarket-orderbook-dataset): public observational archive and documented outages/label censoring; license/schema/provenance must be checked before use.
- [Marketlens publisher archive](https://github.com/marketlenstrade/polymarket-historical-data): books/trades/outcomes across multiple event collections. Its advertised market count cannot be counted directly as independent events.
- [Rocklabs publisher archive](https://github.com/rocklabs-io/polymarket-dataset): very large captured stream archive; downloading it wholesale exceeds this task's bounded acquisition budget.

Publisher archives are legitimate investigation leads, not certified evidence merely because a README says they are historical. No paid download, account bypass, wallet connection or huge archive transfer is performed by this pipeline. Each external archive needs attribution, exact-file hashes, schema and timestamp checks, independent-event grouping, resolution verification and owner-reviewed applicability before it can change any activation result.


## Event-first secondary acquisition

`acquire(event_first=True)` walks bounded closed-event pages instead of sampled market pages. It chooses one supported binary outcome per parent/event group without selecting by eventual payout. Named binary outcomes such as Up/Down retain their actual token/outcome mapping; they are not renamed YES. The same final-resolution and provenance checks apply. A nullable resolution type can qualify only when the two-token payout vector and unique CLOB winner flags explicitly agree; nonbinary named types, empty transaction references and conflicting proofs remain withheld.

The actual secondary acquisition on2026-11-06 obtained250 parent/event metadata groups,51 strict verified outcome groups,616 historical price points across50 groups and95 public METAR observations in132 GET requests/13,369,612 response bytes. It contains0 historical L2 books and0 certifiable events. The other199 resolution rows report explicit final payouts but omit required transaction references; they are not silently upgraded to verified labels. Their nullable type is therefore not the only missing proof. The API payloads remain available for later on-chain corroboration.

The250 distinct IDs are **not**250 independently certified statistical examples. In particular148 records are categorized Up or Down, with likely shared asset, series and adjacent-window dependence. Event-level deduplication removes known multi-leg duplication but cannot prove independence across neighboring crypto windows. Asset/series correlation review, chronological splitting and external context remain necessary. The secondary dataset's raw provenance and normalized manifest are separate from the initial market-window sample; neither is overwritten.

## Public historical L2 acquisition and inspection

A separate bounded research archive was actually downloaded on2026-11-06 from [PolyOrderbooks, Zenodo version22084977](https://zenodo.org/records/22084977), whose metadata and README specify **CC BY4.0**. Attribution: PolyOrderbooks (2026), *Polymarket Crypto Up/Down Order Books: 1-Second L2 Depth Across Three Contract Lengths*. The concept DOI22084114 redirected to that pinned version; exact URLs, response headers, retrieval times and SHA256 hashes are preserved in `data/phase8-public-l2-sample/manifest.json`. The three Parquet files plus metadata, README and a separate single-market JSON sample total48,808,933 response bytes. No publisher code was executed.

Actual schema inspection found897,192 L2 rows,805 distinct market slugs and1,610 token IDs across eight coins:167,941 rows/471 slugs at5minutes;239,310/265 at15minutes;489,941/69 at4hours. Capture timestamps span2026-08-21 through2026-08-24. The schema preserves full bid/ask ladders, sizes, publisher `captured_at`, market end time, token identity and outcome labels. It lacks separate original exchange event timestamps and receipt timestamps. Publisher `winner` and `is_winning_outcome` columns are post-outcome labels and must never enter predictive features. Publisher market-end time is not independently verified final-settlement time.

An independent bounded-batch inspection of every row found20,189 crossed books,52,912 one-sided books and748 captures at or after the listed market end. Crossed flags matched computed best bid/ask comparisons; no mismatched ladder lengths, nonfinite or out-of-range levels, nonpositive sizes, best-quote mismatches or token timestamp reversals were found. These checks do not establish source authenticity or complete capture. Bad execution states remain excluded rather than repaired. The publisher describes WebSocket reconstruction with REST reconciliation every60seconds and discloses dropped-delta drift; a non-crossed row alone cannot prove freshness. Source timestamps and observed receipt proof require further review.

The separate single-market JSON contains600 snapshots and actual metadata market ID3534492, slug`btc-updown-5m-1786596600`, from2026-08-13 04:50–04:55UTC. Its token IDs and provider-attributed capture times remain distinct from official-API settlement proof.

This archive is retained separately from the official-API research bundles and has **not** been normalized into certified books or promoted into strategy validation. Its805 slugs are not805 independent events: adjacent windows for a shared asset and overlapping5minute/15minute/4hour contracts require grouped dependence review. Official event/condition mappings, final transaction-backed resolution corroboration, predecision availability and external context are still missing. It provides genuine candidate L2 observations for further provenance and replay research; it does not remove the activation block or prove that a100/100 independent-event certification dataset is already assembled. `inspection.json` records the actual counts and remaining deficiencies. Reproduce its inspection with optional isolated PyArrow tooling; PyArrow is not introduced into the production dependency set.
